# Efficiency Rules

- **Go direct**: Jump straight to fixing the issue. No lengthy exploration or reading entire files when the problem area is clear.
- **Minimal scope**: Only touch what's needed. Don't refactor, improve, or change anything beyond the specific ask.
- **Ask, don't guess**: If the problem isn't clear, stop and ask for clarification immediately. Don't spend tokens exploring.
- **No browser verification unless asked**: Don't launch browser subagents to check things unless explicitly requested.
- **Short responses**: Keep explanations brief. Say what you changed and why, nothing more.
- **Targeted file reads**: Use grep and line ranges instead of reading entire files. Only read what's relevant.
